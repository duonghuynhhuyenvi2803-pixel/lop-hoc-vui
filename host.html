Prepare your questions, then open the class. Students join on their phones with the QR code or PIN.

⚙️ Settings

This browser has no Vietnamese voice, so commentary will show as subtitles only. Open this page in Microsoft Edge for natural Vietnamese voices (HoaiMy / NamMinh).
Scoring: 20 seconds per question. The fastest correct answer earns points equal to the class size (25 students → 25 points), the second fastest 24, the third 23… Wrong / no answer = 0. After each question everyone changes seats: rank 1 sits closest to the teacher's desk, then left to right, front to back. Two wrong (or blank) answers in a row → stand at the board, still answering; one correct answer sends you back to your seat.
